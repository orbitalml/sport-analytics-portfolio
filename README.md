## Key Findings — Serie A 2024-25
- Average goals per match: 2.56
- Home win rate: 39.7% | Away: 31.8% | Draw: 28.4%
- Most frequent score: 1-1 (53 times)
- Top scorer team: Inter (79 goals)
- Team with most draws: Juventus (16)
- Best recent form (last 5 matchdays): Milan & Roma (12 pts)
